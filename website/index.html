Everything in one place

A complete document workflow

From the scan bed to a shared PDF — every step is built in.

Real scanning

Works with WIA/TWAIN scanners and ADF feeders, plus phone-camera and Phone-to-PC capture.

Smart auto-naming

Reads each page with OCR and remembers the name you gave a document — next time it fills it in for you.

Full page editing

Rotate, deskew, crop, reorder by drag, duplicate, black & white, brightness, sharpen — with undo/redo.

PDF tools

Merge, split, images↔PDF, and compress to a target size. Searchable OCR text baked into your PDFs.

Find anything

Search filenames across all folders, or the text inside your PDFs and images with cached OCR.

Share instantly

Send to WhatsApp, the Windows share sheet, or straight to your phone with a QR code.

Simple by design

From paper to PDF in three steps

Scan or import

Press Scan, drop in files, or snap a photo from your phone. Pages land in the editor instantly.

Tidy up

Auto-crop and deskew, reorder pages, and let ApneScan name the document for you.

Save & share

Export a compact, searchable PDF and send it to WhatsApp, your phone, or any folder.
