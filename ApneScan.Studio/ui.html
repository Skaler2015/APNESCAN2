English & Hindi (Devanagari) recognition data are both bundled — “English + Hindi” reads pages that mix both scripts (Tesseract engine). PaddleOCR AI is a fully-offline neural engine (English/Latin) that reads printed and neat handwriting better; it's slower and, honestly, still won't reliably read a doctor's scrawled prescription — no offline engine does. Auto-naming & type-detection use the chosen engine; searchable-PDF text always uses Tesseract.
